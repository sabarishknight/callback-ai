# 2-minute demo video script

**Setup before recording:** server deployed (or ngrok running), Google Sheet open in one tab, the form in another, the dashboard in a third, your phone on speaker next to the mic, and `TEST_MODE=true` with your own number allowed. Use screen recording plus phone audio. Do one rehearsal call first so Render is awake.

| Time | Screen | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Title slide or the form | "Businesses lose leads because they call back hours later. I built CallBack AI: a voice agent that calls every new enquiry within a minute, qualifies it, and books a meeting." |
| 0:15–0:30 | Fill in the form: your name, your number, "SEO", a short message, tick consent, submit | "This is a demo agency's website. I'll submit an enquiry like a real customer would." Show the thank-you page: "It tells me an AI assistant will call in 30 seconds." |
| 0:30–1:15 | Phone on speaker. Sheet visible in the background. | Let the call play: the AI disclosure, confirming the enquiry, budget, timeline. Ask one FAQ ("Is there a minimum contract?"), then accept a meeting slot. *Tip: answer briefly so the call fits.* |
| 1:15–1:30 | The Google Sheet row updating | "As the call happens, tool calls write to Google Sheets: budget, timeline, the booked slot, and a lead score with its reason, hot, 7 out of 8." |
| 1:30–1:50 | Dashboard: KPIs, outcomes, lead-score chart, hot leads, meetings | "The sales team sees speed-to-call, outcomes, hot leads and upcoming meetings at a glance." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Architecture diagram from the README | "It's built with Vapi, Claude, FastAPI, Google Sheets and Streamlit, with test mode, consent and AI disclosure built in. Thanks for watching." |

**Retake rules:** if the agent mishears, keep going and mention it as a known limitation. Honest beats perfect. Cut dead air in editing.
