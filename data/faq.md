## How much do your services cost?
Social media management is 40,000 to 1,20,000 rupees a month, Google and Meta ads are a 30,000 rupees a month management fee plus ad spend, SEO is 35,000 to 90,000 rupees a month, and websites are 75,000 to 3,00,000 rupees one-time. The exact quote depends on scope and is shared after the strategy call.

## Is there a minimum contract?
Monthly services have a 3-month minimum, because results take time to build. After that it's month to month with 30 days' notice. Websites are a one-time project.

## How soon will we see results?
Ads usually bring leads within the first 2 to 3 weeks. Social media and SEO build steadily, with clear improvement in 2 to 3 months.

## Which industries do you work with?
Mostly D2C brands, restaurants and cafes, clinics, real estate, education and B2B services across India. We've worked with over 120 businesses.

## How will I know it's working?
You get a simple monthly report and a 30-minute review call. Ad clients also get a live dashboard showing spend, leads and cost per lead.

## How do we get started?
Book a free 30-minute strategy call. We review your current marketing, then send a proposal within 2 working days. Work starts about a week after you approve it.
